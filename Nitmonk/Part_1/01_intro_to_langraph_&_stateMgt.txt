LangGraph is a Python framework that helps us build AI applications that work through connected steps and manage information between those steps.

Step 1: User gives a question.
Step 2: AI understands it.
Step 3: AI searches for information.
Step 4: AI generates an answer.

In simple words: LangGraph helps us build AI applications where different tasks work together in an organized way.

What is a Graph?
A graph is a collection of connected steps.

In LangGraph, we have three important things:

Term	Easy meaning

Graph	Complete workflow
Node	One individual task
Edge	Connection between tasks

Remember these three words. They are very important.
